Tips and FAQ

A quick guide to buying, caring for and driving your vehicle in El Salvador. This information is general and does not replace a professional inspection.

What should I check before buying a used car?

Ask for the full paperwork and confirm that the chassis and engine numbers match the documents. Check the body, the tires and the lights, and start the engine cold to listen to how it sounds.

Should I bring a mechanic?

Yes. A check by a trusted mechanic before you pay can catch leaks, hidden damage or transmission problems that are not visible at first glance.

How can I tell if a vehicle had previous damage?

Ask for the vehicle history when it is available, check whether paint or parts show signs of repair, and compare the mileage with the overall condition of the car.

What basic maintenance does any car need?

Oil and filter changes according to the manual, a check of the brakes, fluids and coolant, and a check of the tires and suspension. Keep a record of every service.

Pickup, sedan or SUV?

Choose by use: a pickup for cargo and farm work, a sedan for the city and low fuel use, and an SUV for families and rough roads. Also check which workshops and parts exist for the brand in your area.

What should I check on a used motorcycle?

Check the ownership document and that the serial number matches the motorcycle. Test the brakes and suspension before paying, and make sure you have the license that matches the type of motorcycle.

Tips for driving in the rain and over potholes

Slow down, keep more distance and check your tire pressure. Potholes and flooded streets damage suspension and tires, so it is worth inspecting them after the rainy season.
