What it is for

It gathers pickups, sedans, SUVs, motorcycles and buses, with photos and an explanation of how they are used in the country.

Where the information comes from

The texts are general descriptions based on what is seen and sold in the country. They do not include official figures or cite specific sources. For vehicle fleet statistics, consult the Viceministry of Transport.

The photos

The photos are for reference only and belong to their respective authors.

How it is built

With HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no external libraries. The design is dark, with cards and pill-shaped buttons.
