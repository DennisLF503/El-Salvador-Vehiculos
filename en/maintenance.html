Maintenance reminder

A general list of checks and a reminder for your calendar. The exact intervals, such as how often to change the oil, filters or brake pads, are in your vehicle's manual.

What to check at each service

  • Engine oil and oil filter
  • Air filter
  • Brakes: pads, discs and brake fluid
  • Tires: pressure, wear and rotation
  • Battery and clean terminals
  • Coolant and hoses
  • Lights, wipers and wiper blades
  • Suspension, shock absorbers and steering

This downloads a file you can open with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
